### Types of Database Management Systems We Work With

### Relational Databases (RDBMS)

Traditional structured databases such as MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQL Server used for transactional applications.

### NoSQL Databases

Document-based and key-value stores like MongoDB and Redis, ideal for unstructured or rapidly changing data.

### Cloud-Managed Databases

Hosted database services such as Amazon RDS, Azure SQL, and Firebase with built-in scalability and automated maintenance.

### Distributed Databases

Systems that store data across multiple nodes, such as Cassandra or CockroachDB, for high availability and fault tolerance.

### In-Memory Databases

High-performance databases like Redis or Memcached used to support real-time analytics and caching.

### Industry-Specific Database Solutions

Domain-focused systems designed for sectors such as banking, healthcare, logistics, or telecom with specialised data models and compliance support.
