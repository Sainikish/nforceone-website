**AdTech Infrastructure and Data Pipelines:**
We build scalable, low-latency data pipelines for ad platforms enabling real-time bidding (RTB), campaign tracking, and analytics ingestion. From demand-side platforms (DSPs) to data management platforms (DMPs), we support complex AdTech stacks with high-throughput
cloud-native architectures
**Audience Segmentation and Targeting:**
We implement machine learning models to segment users based on behavior, demographics, and interaction history. This helps advertisers deliver hyper-targeted creatives across devices and optimize spend efficiency.

We enable seamless publishing and content personalization across web, mobile, CTV, and social channels. Our solutions ensure adaptive delivery of media assets with CDN optimization and responsive rendering across devices.
**Real-Time Campaign Analytics and Attribution:**
We integrate real-time dashboards, pixel tracking, and advanced attribution models to measure campaign effectiveness. This empowers marketers to make data-backed decisions, reduce CAC, and improve ROAS.
